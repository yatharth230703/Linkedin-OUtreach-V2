Headline: ⏱️ 180 Minutes. 1 Fiscal Crisis. 1 Roadmap to Resilience.

I’m excited to share my experience at the on-the-spot case competition organized by ASSETS (The Finance and Investment Society of DTU)

With the Union Budget 2026 just days away, this was the ultimate challenge in macro-economic strategy. We had to manage a national budget during a simulated revenue crash caused by a global recession and monsoon failure.

The "Resilience & Renaissance" Strategy 🚀
Approaching this as an optimization problem, our team developed a three-pronged solution:
Grassroots Stability: Reallocated funds to agro-employment while cutting administrative overhead.
Capital Efficiency: Prioritized completing "sunk-cost" projects over starting new ones to ensure liquidity.
Growth Catalysts: Leveraged tax incentives for high-growth sectors like Semiconductors and EVs.

Key Learnings 💡
Fiscal Discipline: Balancing national growth with a strict deficit ceiling is a masterclass in prioritization.
Scenario Planning: In a crisis, the ability to pivot resources toward "multiplier" sectors is what prevents total economic stagnation.
Data-Driven Decisiveness: Under a 3-hour clock, you learn to trust your analysis and make high-stakes calls with confidence.
Collaborative Hustle: Success in case comps depends entirely on how quickly a team can synthesize diverse perspectives into a single, cohesive pitch.

A huge thanks to ASSETS (The Finance and Investment Society of DTU) for an incredible event that served as the perfect "pre-game" for the actual Budget this Sunday.
Shoutout to my teammates Avni Aggarwal and KRISH ANNE, our synergy under pressure made all the difference!
