President, AIMS-DTU.

When I joined AIMS-DTU in my first year, I honestly never thought I’d end up leading the society. This post has been in the pipeline for a long time, and it finally felt like the right moment to put this out.

AIMS-DTU has always been one of the most selective societies at Delhi Technological University (DTU). You don’t just get in and coast. You keep learning, building and improving, or you’re out. Every year, more than 1,000 freshers apply to be a part of AIMS-DTU, with this year’s number touching nearly 1,700.

We started with the orientation and had 700+ students show up. BR Audi was literally overflowing. That kind of energy set the bar early. Since then, our first-years have been deep into ML/DL, doing specialisations, building projects, winning hackathons, and moving fast.

Along the way, we ran Kaggle competitions, Promptify - a prompt engineering event, hosted AI Hack Days by MLH - giving people a chance to build and ship quickly, and so much more... As a society, we went from Smart India Hackathon to IIT Bombay Techfest, picking up accolades along the way. And recently, we pulled off brAInwave 2.0, an MLH-backed event and easily one of the craziest student-run AI hackathons DTU, and honestly North India, has seen to date.

None of this would’ve been possible without the people who made it happen, Ishan Chugh, Hemant Verma, Utkarsh Diwakar, Piyush Gaba, Garvit Singh, Yatharth Bisht for always being involved and taking things forward, my council, Raj Aryan, Hemang Jain, Tarun Jain, Aryan Sood, Sparsh Jain, Siddharth Bansal, Ujjwal Kakar, Shuddhabrota Banerjee, Saksham Jain and our co-heads (too many to name here 😅), and our faculty advisor Dr. Dinesh K. Vishwakarma along with our co-faculty advisors, Dr. Ankit Yadav and Dr. Varsha Sisaudia for their constant guidance and support.

Proud of what we’ve built at AIMS-DTU. Still a long way to go. 🚀

PS: If you’re a company looking to solve real-world problems using AI/ML, we’re always open to collaborating on industrial projects.
