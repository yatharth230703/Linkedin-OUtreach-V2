    I spent my IIT days chasing ₹120.

    • A weird realisation to have years later. Back in the hostel, a few of us had a side hustle. While the ideal students were grinding on LeetCode, 3-4 of us were glued to Chegg. The deal was simple, solve the uploaded questions and get ₹100-120 for each. 5-6 hours each day and it was easy money.

    • Parents never said no for anything, but I hated asking for "fun money." I wanted the leisure, the outings, and the "happening" life without the guilt of asking home for it.

    • Those hours spent on repetitive manual labour were the hours not spent on mastering data structures, doing academics, or securing internships. I still feel that easy money costed me a much bigger jump-start in the corporate.

    • Lesson learnt ?
    If you're a student, don't get seduced by
    "quick" money that doesn't build a skill. The "happening" life is a lot better when you’ve built a career that actually sustains it.

    #IIT #Reflection
