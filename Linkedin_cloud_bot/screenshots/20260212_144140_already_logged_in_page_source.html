I analyse and invest in Indian businesses | x4 exits (food, neurotech, media) | CA (AIR 10) | Ex-Swiggy, Ex- Grant Thornton | Investor, Consultant | Researcher @ Markets by Zerodha

20h •

Around 2 years ago, we went with a simple pitch to Zerodha - "We want to make honest, non-infotainment content around businesses - in Hindi - can you help us?"

How Hindi was seen by marketing teams of other brokers at that time is a conversation for another day.

But Zerodha showed faith in us, and doubled down by figuring all the peripheral stuff around video creation (editing, uploading etc). Even when most of their team members were non-native Hindi speakers, they understood the gap in smart content for Hindi speaking audience.

Today, Markets by Zerodha Hindi has a separate fan base.

It has grown to ~ 1.6L subscribers.

We work exclusively with Zerodha - and when we agreed to this, neither of us knew where this would go. But what made us take that leap of faith was that Zerodha team NEVER asked us to sell anything for them - even subtly (you know how big brands can make you do it!).

And we are proud to be a part of this ongoing market creation journey.

This niche in smart Hindi content was non existent two years ago.

Other brands are catching up now. And I think that's really good for Indian retail investors in general. I genuinely hope YT is flooded with awesome educational content around markets in Hindi.

It is high time that native Hindi speaking audience isn't seen as "subscriber count farm" and gets more and more smart content made for them, instead of just "aaj ka price action dekho" ya "stock market se 1 crore kamao" kinda content.

And we are happy to lead the change alongside Zerodha :)

P.S: Special thanks to Sagar and Bhuvanesh for nudging us in this direction!

    I spent my IIT days chasing ₹120.

    • A weird realisation to have years later. Back in the hostel, a few of us had a side hustle. While the ideal students were grinding on LeetCode, 3-4 of us were glued to Chegg. The deal was simple, solve the uploaded questions and get ₹100-120 for each. 5-6 hours each day and it was easy money.

    • Parents never said no for anything, but I hated asking for "fun money." I wanted the leisure, the outings, and the "happening" life without the guilt of asking home for it.

    • Those hours spent on repetitive manual labour were the hours not spent on mastering data structures, doing academics, or securing internships. I still feel that easy money costed me a much bigger jump-start in the corporate.

    • Lesson learnt ?
    If you're a student, don't get seduced by
    "quick" money that doesn't build a skill. The "happening" life is a lot better when you’ve built a career that actually sustains it.

    #IIT #Reflection

    1️⃣ Show Passion ↳ People connect when you show genuine enthusiasm. ↳ If you don't care about what you're saying, neither will they. 2️⃣ Tell Them Stories ↳ Storytelling keeps ideas fresh. ↳ Share the process behind your thinking or what you learned. 3️⃣ The "Three" Rule ↳ Our brains love patterns. ↳ Structure your message around three key takeaways. 4️⃣ Keep it Simple ↳ You don't need jargon to make a strong message. ↳ Use an image or short phrase instead of long explanations. 5️⃣ Bring the Villain ↳ Every good message needs tension. ↳ Name the challenge, obstacle, or problem you're solving. 6️⃣ Bring the Hero ↳ After you've shown the problem, introduce the solution. ↳ Give people an action to rally around. (Pro tip: They're the hero, not you.) 7️⃣ Humanize the Data ↳ This ties into storytelling. Explain why people should care. ↳ Instead of "10GB of storage", Steve said, "Room for 1,000 songs in your pocket." 8️⃣ The Holy Sh!t Moment ↳ Create a memorable moment. ↳ Show people why your message matters. 9️⃣ One Sentence Summary ↳ Steve created a trick called the "Twitter-friendly headline." ↳ If you can't distill your message into one sentence, it's not clear enough. These techniques develop your confidence in your speech, and over time, that confidence turns into influence. Strong influence makes it easier to rally people around your ideas and create the impact you're after.
