I analyse and invest in Indian businesses | x4 exits (food, neurotech, media) | CA (AIR 10) | Ex-Swiggy, Ex- Grant Thornton | Investor, Consultant | Researcher @ Markets by Zerodha

20h •

Around 2 years ago, we went with a simple pitch to Zerodha - "We want to make honest, non-infotainment content around businesses - in Hindi - can you help us?"

How Hindi was seen by marketing teams of other brokers at that time is a conversation for another day.

But Zerodha showed faith in us, and doubled down by figuring all the peripheral stuff around video creation (editing, uploading etc). Even when most of their team members were non-native Hindi speakers, they understood the gap in smart content for Hindi speaking audience.

Today, Markets by Zerodha Hindi has a separate fan base.

It has grown to ~ 1.6L subscribers.

We work exclusively with Zerodha - and when we agreed to this, neither of us knew where this would go. But what made us take that leap of faith was that Zerodha team NEVER asked us to sell anything for them - even subtly (you know how big brands can make you do it!).

And we are proud to be a part of this ongoing market creation journey.

This niche in smart Hindi content was non existent two years ago.

Other brands are catching up now. And I think that's really good for Indian retail investors in general. I genuinely hope YT is flooded with awesome educational content around markets in Hindi.

It is high time that native Hindi speaking audience isn't seen as "subscriber count farm" and gets more and more smart content made for them, instead of just "aaj ka price action dekho" ya "stock market se 1 crore kamao" kinda content.

And we are happy to lead the change alongside Zerodha :)

P.S: Special thanks to Sagar and Bhuvanesh for nudging us in this direction!
