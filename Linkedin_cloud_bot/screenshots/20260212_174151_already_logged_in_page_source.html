My November visit to India coincided with an exciting milestone – JPMorganChase announced #Pune as our newest Global Technology Center. This marks a new chapter for our firm and especially our tech teams in India.

The inaugural celebration was led by Rohan Amin, Chase Chief Product Officer, Oron Gill Haus, Chase Chief Information Officer, Divjot Nanda, head of technology for Chase Travel Business Services and Pune Tech Center lead, and Deepak Mangla, CEO, Corporate Centers in India and the Philippines, with participation from other JPMorganChase India leaders. Pune has contributed to JPMorganChase’s technology landscape since 2021, and its new status as a Global Technology Center underscores the significance of the work being done there. Pune now joins Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Mumbai as a JPMorganChase Global Technology Center in India.

Our 23 technology centers in key markets worldwide provide global coverage and a resilient, diverse team. During my visit, I saw first-hand the creative solutions India teams are developing. By bringing together top talent in India, we empower our people to make a real impact for our clients and communities.

As Pune teams embrace their new status, the focus remains on driving innovation, supporting employee growth and delivering exceptional value to our clients and communities. The work in Pune will continue to play a vital role in shaping the future of JPMorganChase in India and around the world.

Congratulations to our Pune colleagues and everyone who made this milestone possible!
