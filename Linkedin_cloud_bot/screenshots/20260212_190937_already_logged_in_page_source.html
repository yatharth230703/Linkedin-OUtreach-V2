Announcement for 2026 programme

The NIUS programme organized by the Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education (HBCSE-TIFR), Mumbai is designed to provide extended mentoring and guidance to undergraduate students in various areas of science. This is the first announcement for the NIUS programme in Astronomy for the year 2026.

The broad structure of the NIUS (Astronomy) programme is as follows. All selected students will attend an exposure and enrichment camp to be held from 17 to 28 July 2026 at the HBCSE campus in Mumbai

Applications are invited for NIUS (ASTRONOMY) for 2026 from FIRST-YEAR undergraduate students with a strong interest in scientific research and development, especially in Astronomy and Astrophysics, who are enrolled in India for any of the following degrees (categorised into four groups):

Group (i): 3-year or 4-year B.Sc./ B.Sc.(H)/B.S. with Physics as one of the subjects,
Group (ii): 4-year B. Tech./B.E. in Physics/Applied Physics/Engineering Physics,
Group (iii): 5-year BS-MS/Integrated M. Sc.in Physics/Astronomy, and
Group (iv): 4-year/5-year B.E./B.Tech./B.Tech-M.Tech Dual Degree in any branch of Engineering/Technology.

Applicants should have a minimum of 60% marks or equivalent grades in Class X and Class XII examinations.

Deadline for submission of applications by candidates: 05 March 2026, 23:59 IST

I got both my jobs at Citi and JPMorganChase through referrals.

That’s why I say:
Network like it’s your second job.

But real networking isn’t:
Can you find any opening for me?
Can you send my resume to the HR?
Sending your CV in the first message

That’s not networking.
That’s panic.

Real networking looks like:
Genuine curiosity about someone’s journey
Thoughtful follow-ups (without spamming)
Staying in touch even when you don’t need anything
Adding value via a perspective, a thank you, a meaningful comment

Let me also say this honestly:
You will reach out to people and not get a reply.
Not because they don’t care.
Not because you said something wrong.
But because inboxes are flooded.

Mine is too.

So don’t take silence personally.
And please don’t stop reaching out.
Keep showing up.
Keep reaching out to more people.
Give luck more chances to find you.

Because in the end, people don’t refer resumes - they refer people they remember and trust.

If this helped, there’s more where that came from!!
Follow Vishal Jalan for such helpful insights.
