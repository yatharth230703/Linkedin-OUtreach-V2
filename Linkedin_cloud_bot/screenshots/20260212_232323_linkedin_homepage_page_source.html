1d •

Gen Z consumers gravitate towards brands that feel relatable rather than perfect, and 87% choose brands that hold personal meaning for them, a combined report by The Economic Times, Snapchat and Kantar suggests.

Based on insights collected over the course of 2025, the report shares that authenticity is non-negotiable for Gen Z. Unfiltered, honest recommendations rank high over polished advertising for this cohort, with 84% following influencers who share real experiences. Around 79% are open to niche or lesser-known brands, and 88% take pride in homegrown brands.

Calling “deal-hunting” a lifestyle, the report suggests that 93% of the cohort aim to find best deals online and offline.

In terms of work life, flexibility is with 35% of those surveyed preferring the hybrid model, while 17% opting for work-from-home, and 26% preferring work-from-office. While salary is the primary driver, work-life balance, job security, and passion fulfillment also make the cut.

Education is viewed as an investment rather than a fixed pathway with 77% pursuing higher education and 67% aiming to study abroad.
