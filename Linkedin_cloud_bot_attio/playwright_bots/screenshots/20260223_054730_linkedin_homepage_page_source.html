AI & Research @ Lexsi

3d •

Wrapped up an exciting week in New Delhi at the India AI Impact Summit 2026, representing Lexsi Labs!

Day 1 was a defining moment - I had the honour of meeting Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi. He inaugurated the Summit, setting the tone for showcasing India’s AI innovation and research on a global stage; right here at home 🇮🇳

Day 2 concluded with us hosting “AGI Talks: A Night with AI Researchers” - a successful meetup with discussions on AGI, RL, interpretability and foundational modeling alongside a room full of like-minded individuals 🚀

Personal favourites were the keynotes at the Research Symposium on AI and It’s Impact from the likes of Demis Hassabis, Yann LeCun and Yoshua Bengio around alignment, AGI, and world models; just a regular Tuesday talking about machines getting smarter than us :)

All thanks to Lexsi Labs for the opportunity of a lifetime. Loved meeting and connecting with everyone who stopped by our booth and engaged with our work. Big releases coming soon 👀
