Built something for months. Defended it in minutes.

A slightly late post, but definitely not a forgotten one.

Smart India Hackathon 2025 Finale happened in December, and I am finally reflecting on it in February.

Our team built a transformer based Web Application Firewall aimed at detecting zero day exploits using anomaly detection instead of signatures. We focused on making it practical, fast, and capable of adapting to new traffic patterns in real time. During evaluation, the system maintained strong detection performance with an average latency of around 45 ms per request on a laptop GPU, which made the discussions around real world deployment even more exciting.

The real takeaway was the experience. Presenting under pressure, defending architectural decisions in front of experts, and refining the system through long days made it feel like we were shipping something real.

One of the most meaningful moments was interacting with scientists from ISRO - Indian Space Research Organization's Space Applications Centre. Those conversations went far beyond the competition and opened doors I am genuinely excited about.

On behalf of Team M3OW, I would also like to sincerely thank Gulshan Gupta sir from ISRO for his mentorship, guidance during the hackathon, and continued support afterward.

Grateful to my teammates Arnabi Dutta, Arsh Abbas Naqvi, Aryan Goel, Arjun Tomar and Vedant Krish Shanker for the grind and the belief also thankful to the EHAX community for pushing our interest in web security and networks in the right direction.Walking away from SIH more confident and even more motivated to keep building.
