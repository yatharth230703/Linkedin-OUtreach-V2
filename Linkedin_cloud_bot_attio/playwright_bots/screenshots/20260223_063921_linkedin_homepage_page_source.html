From learning to leaderboard — secured 4th rank in the AIMS Kaggle Competition 2k26!
I’m excited to share that I finished 4th in the AIMS Kaggle Competition 2k26!
This competition turned out to be one of the most valuable hands-on learning experiences in my AI/ML journey.
What made this special wasn’t just the rank, but the end-to-end exposure to building an AI model. From understanding the problem statement and performing exploratory data analysis, to data preprocessing, feature engineering, model selection, validation, and iterative improvements — every step taught me something new.

What I learned along the way:
• How small data preprocessing decisions can significantly impact model performance
• The importance of proper validation and avoiding overfitting
• Experimenting with multiple models and tuning them systematically
• Thinking like a problem-solver, not just a coder

Competing alongside so many talented participants and learning from shared discussions and notebooks made this journey even more rewarding.
Grateful to the organizers and the Kaggle community for creating such a great learning platform. Looking forward to applying these skills to more real-world problems and competitions!
