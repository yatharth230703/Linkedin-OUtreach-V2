Excited to have recently started my SDE Internship at Oracle! 🔴

Reflecting on the process, I wanted to share my interview experience and key takeaways. While I can't share the exact code, the rounds highlighted exactly what top-tier tech companies are looking for right now. It wasn't just about grinding LeetCode; it was a test of true engineering breadth. 🧠

Here are the 3 pillars that defined my rounds:

1. CS Fundamentals are Non-Negotiable 🏗️ We moved beyond the IDE. A significant portion of my rounds (especially Rounds 1 & 2) focused heavily on the "invisible" parts of software:

Operating Systems 🖥️
Computer Networks 🌐
DBMS 🗄️

👉 Takeaway: Knowing how to write code is good; knowing how the machine executes it is better.

2. The "Why" Behind the Data Structure ⚡ Yes, there were standard DSA challenges (like Trapping Rainwater 🌧️ and Graph traversals 📊). But the focus wasn't just on getting a solution.

Optimization: How do you handle memory?
Edge Cases: What if the input breaks standard types?

👉 Highlight: They want to see efficiency, not just memorization.

3. Thought Process > Syntax 🧠 My final round was the standout. I was asked to solve a "Perfect Square" problem, but the interviewer wasn't looking for a quick one-liner.

We generated test cases 🧪
We validated assumptions ✅
We handled massive inputs (think infinite digits ♾️)

👉 Highlight: It wasn't about just solving the problem; it was about the rigor. How do you verify your answer? It proved that Oracle values the engineering mindset—the "how you think"—above all else.

💡 My advice for future applicants?
Don't just practice coding; practice testing your code. A strong grasp of fundamentals and a structured thought process will save you when the question gets tricky.
