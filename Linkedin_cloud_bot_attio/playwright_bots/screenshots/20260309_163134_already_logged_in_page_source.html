      Startups That Are Hiring Interns & Entry-Level Talent !!!

      When I first wanted to break into the startup world, the best advice I received was simple:

      👉 Work at a startup early to see if it’s actually a good fit for you.

      So for all the students and recent grads looking to explore startups while still in school (or just starting out), this list is for you:

      • Samira Behrouzan from a16z speedrun is hiring an Events & Community Marketing Lead to produce founder events and build the community engine behind their early-stage accelerator.

      • Laura Frishman from Garage (YC W24) is hiring across GTM (AE, Growth Ops, Client Ops), Engineering (SWE), Design, and Recruiting to scale its AI-powered marketplace for essential vehicles.

      • Michelle Volberg from Twill is hiring a Founding BDR (fully remote, US) to help scale its referral-based recruiting platform.

      • Siddhi Mittal from yhangry (YC W22) is hiring one paid AI Intern to build and ship AI agents with Claude at her YC-backed chef marketplace (London).

      • Nali Sutipayakul from Creative People is hiring a NYC-based Social Media Specialist ($40/hr, 15 hrs/week) for a 1-month hybrid project focused on short-form video and content design.

      • Zainabou Thiam from Sunu Body is hiring PR & Influencer Outreach and Email Marketing Interns to help grow a modern African self-care brand rooted in ancestral wellness.

      • Hana Elster from VIA is hiring student ambassadors to create TikTok content and help launch her secondhand fashion discovery platform (no experience required).

      • Arshdeep Toor from Stesh Butter is hiring a Social Media & Marketing Intern (remote, flexible) to create short-form content and help grow her better-for-you pistachio butter brand.

      • Cherie Chung from Holly (gov workforce AI) is hiring a Senior Software Engineer in NYC or SF to build AI tools for government compensation and classification.

      • Rebeccah H. from Kneupper & Covey PC is hiring a part-time AI Intern (10–20 hrs/week, remote) to build and scale AI systems for a consumer-focused law firm.

      • Jillian McIntyre from 221B Capital Partners is hiring two Summer 2026 interns for a woman-majority-owned global long/short hedge fund focused on technology, renewable energy, and industrials.

      • Sapien is hiring their first Community Lead to design and scale the community engine behind their AI platform for finance and operations leaders.

      • Encore (YC F24) is hiring a Core Product Designer and a Software Engineering Intern in SF to help scale Candle, their relationship-focused app designed to help couples connect.

      🔁 Repost to help another student land their first startup role.

      🚀 If you’re a founder hiring interns or entry-level talent, DM me your open role, I’d love to include it in my next list.
