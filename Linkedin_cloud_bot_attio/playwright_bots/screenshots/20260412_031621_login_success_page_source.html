“I have strong projects”
👉 We need DSA

“I solved 1000+ problems”
👉 We need experience

“I did internships”
👉 We need open source

“I did everything…”
👉 We’re moving forward with other candidates 💀

Here’s the truth no one tells you 🧠

There is no perfect checklist.

Because hiring isn’t just about
skills, certificates, or numbers

It’s about fit, timing, and positioning

You can do everything right
and still hear “no”

Not because you’re not good enough
but because the process is… unpredictable

So what should you actually do?

👉 Build real projects
👉 Practice problem solving
👉 Share your work publicly
👉 Keep applying consistently

And most importantly
👉 Don’t tie your worth to rejections

Because every “no” is not failure
It’s just redirection 🚀

Stay in the game long enough
and one “yes” will change everything

    iDEX Defence Exploration and Learning (iDEAL) - Internship Program 2026 - DEfense Innovation Organisation (DIO) - Dept.of Defense Production - Ministry of Defense - Govt.of India , India ::

    iDEX Defence Exploration and Learning (iDEAL) is a stipend based 3-month and 6- month internship program which aims to provide exposure to the students, along with practical experience in managing projects focusing on niche technology development in the areas of Defence and Aerospace.

    Who can apply?

    i. iDEAL internship program at DIO shall generally be open to Indian citizens with specified educational qualification from a recognized University.

    ii. The age of the candidate should not exceed 26 years as on 30-04-2026.

    iii. Undergraduate students in their final year or graduated from degree program for science and engineering/technology in the year of application.

    iv. Postgraduate students, pursuing or graduated in the year of application with bachelors’ applicability as per pt. iii

    v. Candidates who have undergone or undergoing Apprenticeship Training under NATS or NAPS in any other establishment/organization are not eligible.
    vi. Suppression of facts & furnishing false information will lead to disqualification/Rejection of candidature.

    Canvassing in any form will result in disqualification.

    What will you gain?

    i. Stipend: iDEAL interns will be eligible for a monthly stipend of INR 30,000/- per month for 6-month internship period, and INR 20,000/- per month for 3-month internship period (Maximum duration of internship can be extended on need basis).

    ii. Internship Experience Certificate: iDEAL interns will be eligible to receive a certificate of experience issued by iDEX/MoD upon successful completion of the internship program.

    iii. Hands-on Experience: Interns would be provided with comprehensive Defence related technology project management experience. By engaging in diverse responsibilities and receiving regular evaluations and feedback, interns will develop valuable skills and gain exposure to real-world projects.

    Place of Internship

    i. All interns will be required to report at DIO office located at 8 th Floor, Konnectus Towers - II, Bhavabhuti Marg, New Delhi, 110002.

    Last date of applying for internship :: 30th April 2026
