Gamifying Maths | | Engineering @ Matiks | | One of the Youngest SDE

1w •

Back in August 2024, I joined Matiks with a simple belief : learn, build, and grow.
By October 2024, when I moved to Bangalore, I was told:
“Bas 2 mahine ka kaam hai… then things will be smooth, just small bug fixes, UI tweaks, and minor changes.”
Fast forward to today, it’s been more than 1.5 years.
And guess what?
Those “2 months” never really came.
But something else did.
Growth.
When I started, our dev team was just 3 people. The product had barely 5–6 routes simple, early-stage, full of possibilities.
Today?
The team has scaled 5X
The product has evolved into a complex platform
Multiple formats, multiple features, multiple experiences, all built step by step
And me?
I evolved with it.
From working on basic landing pages to contributing across a growing system, solving real problems, adapting constantly, and learning things no roadmap could have predicted.
Yes, the hours didn’t change much.
But the person doing those hours did.
Sometimes, the journey isn’t what you were promised.
It’s better.
Because instead of getting “easy work after 2 months,”
I got 1.5 years of continuous learning, ownership, and real-world building.
And honestly, I wouldn’t trade that.
Still building. Still learning.
