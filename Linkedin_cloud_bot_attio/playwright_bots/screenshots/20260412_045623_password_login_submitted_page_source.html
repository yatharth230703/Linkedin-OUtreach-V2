    🚨 I Almost Fell for This Internship Trap 🚨

    A few days ago, I received an offer for a Data Analyst Internship with a ₹40,000/month stipend and a fully remote setup.
    Sounds amazing, right?
    That’s exactly what I thought.
    But something felt off… so I decided to dig deeper.

    Here’s what I found:
    ❌ No proper interview process
    ❌ Offer rolled out too quickly
    ❌ Very high stipend for a fresher role
    ❌ Limited credible company presence
    ❌ Questionable employee profiles

    At that moment, I realized — not every opportunity is genuine.
    I chose to step back.
    And honestly, that decision might have saved me from a bad situation.

    💡 Lesson:
    If something feels too good to be true, take a pause and verify.
    Before accepting any offer:
    ✔ Check company registration (MCA/GST)
    ✔ Review employee profiles carefully
    ✔ Look for a proper hiring process
    ✔ Trust your instincts

    We’re all working hard to build our careers — don’t let shortcuts or fake offers derail your journey.
    If this helps even one person avoid a mistake, it’s worth sharing.
    Stay alert. Stay smart.
