Apple and I really have a thing at this point...

I left Apple to explore, build, and figure out what I actually want to work on.
Guess who’s back? Back again.

Through Carnegie Mellon University, I got to go deeper into AI, experiment, build things from scratch, and understand which problems are actually worth solving. Somewhere along the way, things started to make more sense, and interestingly, that journey brought me back to where a lot of it started.

This summer, I’ll be joining Apple San Diego as an intern one more time. Coming back feels different. Not just familiar, but more intentional. I’m coming back with a clearer sense of what I want to work on and what I can bring to the table.

Grateful for my parents, my friends, and everyone who backed me through the uncertain parts. Big thanks to Jen R. for making the hiring process super smooth. Special shoutout to Hasita Bommakanti and Om Kulkarni for being there every step of the way :)

Really excited to be part of the Apple family again. Good to be back. 🍎
