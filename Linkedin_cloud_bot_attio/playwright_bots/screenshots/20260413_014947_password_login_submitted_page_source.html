**How to Check Whether a Job/Internship Opportunity is Real or Fake**

In today’s job market, getting an offer sometimes feels like winning the lottery. But before celebrating, do a quick **“Scam Reality Check”**:

**Step 1:**
If the company offers **₹40K–₹1L stipend for an intern without even interviewing you**… congratulations 🎉
You didn’t get a job.
You got **selected for a scam experiment.**

**Step 2:**
Check the **company website.**
If it looks like it was built in **30 minutes using free templates**, be careful.

**Step 3:**
Open LinkedIn and check the **employees.**
If everyone joined the company **last week** and their photos look like they came from an **AI image generator**, run. 🏃‍♂️

**Step 4:**
Check the **email domain.**
If the offer letter says `company.com` but the email comes from `company.hr@gmail.com`…
That’s not HR.
That’s **Hustle & Robbery Department.**

**Step 5:**
If they say:
“Buy a laptop / software and we will reimburse later.”

Translation:
**You buy it. They disappear.**

💡 **Golden rule:**
If the opportunity feels **too good to be true**, it usually is.

Stay safe, verify companies properly, and don’t let scammers turn your job search into their side business.

#JobSearch #InternshipScam #CareerTips #Freshers #StayAlert
