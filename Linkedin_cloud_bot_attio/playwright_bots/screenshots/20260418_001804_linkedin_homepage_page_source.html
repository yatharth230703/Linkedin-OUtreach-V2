Summer Research Internship Program (SRIP 2026) - Institute of Infrastructure, Technology, Research And Management (IITRAM) - Gujarat, India ::

The Institute of Infrastructure, Technology, Research And Management (IITRAM) has been established by the Government of Gujarat as an Autonomous University and has been mandated to bring about significant change in Engineering Education with respect to Technical and Managerial knowledge in the area of Infrastructure.

The Institute offers Bachelor and Master of Technology programs in Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering Electrical Engineering, and Computer Science Engineering. The Institute also offers Ph.D. studies in various fields in Basic Sciences, Engineering, Humanities and Social Sciences.

IITRAM invites applications for the ‘Summer Research Internship Program (SRIP 2026)’ to provide research exposure to undergraduate and postgraduate students from IITRAM and other Institutions.

Objectives of SRIP 2026

• To provide early exposure to research and innovation in emerging areas of engineering, science, and technology
• To enable students to work closely with experienced faculty mentors on real-world research problems
• To enhance analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and research aptitude • To encourage students to pursue higher studies and research-oriented careers
• To promote inter-institutional collaboration and academic exchange • To facilitate the development of technical writing, presentation, and professional skills

Internship Details

Duration : Minimum 01 month, maximum 02 months
Period : 15 May 2026 – 20 July 2026

Positions : 35 internships

Stipend : ₹5,000 per month (up to 1 or 2 months) for funded projects only.
• Subsidized Hostel accommodation may be provided, if available.
• Temporary Institute ID for access to Library, Internet, and departmental facilities.

Eligibility Criteria
• Pre-final /Final year students of B.Tech./M.Tech. programs - (for more information refer Annexure-1)
• Strong academic record, Statement of Purpose (SOP), and research credentials
• Students with backlogs or disciplinary records are not eligible

Last date for Online Application is 30/04/2026 at 18:00 Hrs.
