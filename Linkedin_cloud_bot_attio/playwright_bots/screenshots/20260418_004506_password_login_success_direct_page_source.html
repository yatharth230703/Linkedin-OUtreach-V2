🚨 Awareness Post: Be Careful with Internship Offers
I recently received an internship offer from a company named Bold Analytics for a Data Analyst Intern role.
At first glance, the offer looked professional, but after reviewing it carefully, I noticed several red flags:
❌ No clear mention of stipend
❌ Very generic job description
❌ No proper interview or selection process
❌ Focus on certificate and LOR instead of real work
❌ Limited company verification and online presence
Many students and freshers may fall for such offers, especially when they are eager to gain experience.
⚠️ Important advice:
Do not pay any fees for internships
Verify the company through LinkedIn and official websites
Be cautious before sharing personal information
Let’s stay informed and help each other avoid fake or low-quality opportunities.
#InternshipScam #FakeOffer #StudentAwareness #CareerTips #LinkedInIndia

      🚨 Important Awareness for Freshers & Internship Seekers

      I recently received an offer for a “Data Analyst Intern” role from a fintech company. Initially, it looked like a great opportunity—but after reviewing it carefully, I noticed a few concerning points.

      🔍 Observations:
      • The internship is unpaid for a technical role like Data Analyst
      • No proper HR contact details (no name or phone number)
      • Offer acceptance through a clickable button instead of a formal signed document
      • Email format includes an “unsubscribe” option (unusual for official offers)
      • No clear interview or selection process mentioned

      ⚠️ Additionally, I came across discussions online where some candidates mentioned being asked to pay a small “certificate fee” during onboarding. This is something to be cautious about.

      💡 My takeaway:
      Before accepting any opportunity,
      • Verify the company’s presence on LinkedIn
      • Check real employees and their activity
      • Avoid sharing personal documents without confirmation
      • Never pay for internships unless it’s a clearly structured course/training

      I’m sharing this purely for awareness so that others don’t rush into decisions.

      If anyone has genuine experience with similar offers, feel free to share your thoughts.

      #Internship #JobSearch #Freshers #CareerAwareness #DataAnalytics #JobTips
