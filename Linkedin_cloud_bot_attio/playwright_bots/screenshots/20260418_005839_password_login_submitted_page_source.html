      🚨 Beware of Fake Internship Offers 🚨

      Recently, I came across an internship offer that made me realize how important it is to stay cautious during the job search process.

      Despite not having any proper interview and even performing poorly in the assignment, I still received an offer with a high stipend. This raised serious concerns and made me dig deeper — and I realized it was likely a fake or untrustworthy opportunity.

      💡 Here are some red flags I noticed:

      - No proper interview or evaluation process
      - Selection despite weak performance
      - Unrealistically high stipend for an intern role
      - Immediate offer without meaningful interaction

      ⚠️ Many such opportunities are designed to:

      - Collect personal data
      - Make candidates work without pay
      - Or even ask for money later under different excuses

      ✅ Always verify before accepting any offer:

      - Check company presence on LinkedIn and official websites
      - Connect with current employees
      - Never pay any fees for internships or jobs
      - Trust your instincts if something feels off

      Let’s stay aware and help each other avoid such scams.

      #Internship #JobSearch #CareerAdvice #FakeInternship #ScamAlert #Students #Freshers
