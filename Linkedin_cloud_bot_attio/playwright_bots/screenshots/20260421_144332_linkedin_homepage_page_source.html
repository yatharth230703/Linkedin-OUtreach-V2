I cleared 5 rounds for the SWE 1 role at Intuit and I’m genuinely proud of myself.
After clearing 5 rounds, I was invited for the final round at Intuit. However, before the interview could be scheduled, I was informed that the hiring for this cycle had been closed.

At first, I felt disappointed. But then I remembered how confidently I handled the recruiter 1:1 and the tech screen. That gave me a fresh perspective.
This entire interview process taught me so much:
Summarizing your entire 1 year of experience + college background in just 30 minutes with a recruiter sounds easy, but it actually takes a lot of preparation and finesse.

I got to passionately share how I use AI tools in my day-to-day life. The conversation felt more like a discussion with an experienced senior rather than an interview, and Intuit’s team seemed genuinely interested.

The Build Challenge helped me revise all my low-level and high-level design concepts, from Single Responsibility Principle (SRP) to Content Delivery Network (CDN). I learned how to take a small project and make it production-ready by thinking deeply about trade-offs and long-term success.

I realized that creating something and maintaining it for the long term are two completely different skills. This experience taught me true code ownership and the importance of documentation.

Most importantly, this process has changed how I approach my day-to-day work. I’ve started documenting the bugs I fix and the problems I solve, and I’ve become much more aware of core AI concepts like LLMs, RAG, and Agentic AI.

Thank you, Intuit, for this valuable learning opportunity. I’ll continue to grow and look forward to the possibility of working with you in the future.
Rejections are tough, but the growth they bring is truly priceless.

Delighted to share that our team “Green Pulse” from Delhi Technological University (DTU) has emerged as the winner of Harit Manthan 2026 – National Hackathon, organized by the Delhi Development Authority (DDA) in collaboration with Udhmodya Foundation, securing up to ₹10 lakhs of seed funding support! 🌿🏆

Our solution focuses on smart monitoring of urban green spaces using IoT sensors, converting environmental data into a Green Space Health Index (GSHI) for real-time insights, smarter decision-making, and efficient park management. We developed a working prototype demonstrating real-time sensing and actionable analytics for on-ground impact.

We were honored to present our work in the presence of esteemed dignitaries. Special thanks to Dr. Vipul Aggarwal (Principal Commissioner, Horticulture/Landscape, DDA) for setting the vision and context, and Shri N. Saravana Kumar (Vice Chairman, DDA) for his insightful address.
It was a privilege to attend the keynote by Shri Taranjit Singh Sandhu (Hon’ble Lieutenant Governor, Delhi), which truly inspired us to think about scalable, national-level impact.

A heartfelt thanks to our mentor Dr. Arjun Tyagi for guiding us throughout the journey with valuable insights and constant support. His inputs played a crucial role in shaping our solution.

Huge congratulations to my amazing teammates Hardik Garg and Dhruv Dawar for this incredible achievement! Going forward, we are excited to collaborate with DDA to pilot Green Pulse in urban parks to help authorities improve green space quality at scale. 🚀🌱
