We recently won the Paytm Fin-O-Hack organized by ASSETS (The Finance and Investment Society of DTU)

The problem statement pushed us to rethink how users actually interact with a super app like Paytm.

While working on it, we realized something interesting — Paytm already offers 30+ services like travel, insurance, investments, and more, but most users primarily use it for UPI.
The gap isn’t in features, but in how users discover and access them.

💡 Our solution focused on building an **intelligent intent-driven system**:
Instead of a single chatbot interface, we designed a system where users can interact through voice or text, and based on their intent, they are seamlessly directed to the relevant service within the app.

For example:

* “Ma ko ₹500 bhejo” → directly triggers the payments flow
* “Plan a trip to Manali” → opens a curated travel planning interface

Behind the scenes, this is powered by specialized agents that understand the task and route it to the correct workflow, making the experience faster and more intuitive without changing how users already behave.

And thank you to my incredible team Prisha Gulati Akansh Gupta and Ishansh Sharma — couldn’t have done this without you .

Grateful for the experience given by ASSETS (The Finance and Investment Society of DTU) and everything we learned along the way.
