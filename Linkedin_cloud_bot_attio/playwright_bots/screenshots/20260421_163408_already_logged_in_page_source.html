    AIMS-DTU

    2w • Edited •

    In December 2025, we took part in Techfest, IIT Bombay 2025, proudly representing Delhi Technological University (Formerly DCE) as AIMS-DTU under the guidance of Dr. Dinesh K. Vishwakarma. From datathons and GeoAI challenges to product and fintech competitions, our teams delivered strong performances across diverse domains once again showcasing our depth in AI/ML, systems thinking, and real-world problem solving.

    AIMS-DTU continues to stand out as a student-driven community that blends deep technical expertise with strong execution and collaboration. Across national platforms like Techfest, our members consistently demonstrate the ability to move from ideas to deployable solutions, whether in AI research, systems design, product thinking, or policy-driven impact problems. These results reflect not just individual brilliance, but a culture of rigor, mentorship, and teamwork that enables AIMS-DTU to compete, adapt, and excel across domains far beyond the classroom.
