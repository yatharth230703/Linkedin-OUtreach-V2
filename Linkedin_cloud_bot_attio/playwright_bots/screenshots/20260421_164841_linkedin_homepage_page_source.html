AIMS-DTU

2w • Edited •

In December 2025, we took part in Techfest, IIT Bombay 2025, proudly representing Delhi Technological University (Formerly DCE) as AIMS-DTU under the guidance of Dr. Dinesh K. Vishwakarma. From datathons and GeoAI challenges to product and fintech competitions, our teams delivered strong performances across diverse domains once again showcasing our depth in AI/ML, systems thinking, and real-world problem solving.

AIMS-DTU continues to stand out as a student-driven community that blends deep technical expertise with strong execution and collaboration. Across national platforms like Techfest, our members consistently demonstrate the ability to move from ideas to deployable solutions, whether in AI research, systems design, product thinking, or policy-driven impact problems. These results reflect not just individual brilliance, but a culture of rigor, mentorship, and teamwork that enables AIMS-DTU to compete, adapt, and excel across domains far beyond the classroom.

𝗭𝗲𝗿𝘃𝗲𝗔𝗜 (𝗛𝗲𝗮𝗹𝘁𝗵 𝗜𝗻𝘀𝘂𝗿𝗮𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗗𝗮𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗵𝗼𝗻)
🥇 1st Position – Ishansh Sharma, Shivaay Dhondiyal, Ridhwan Deshwal, Akansh Gupta
Built robust ML pipelines on 450k+ imbalanced rows using LightGBM with advanced feature engineering and ensembling (CatBoost + XGBoost), achieving ~0.28 normalized Gini.


𝗡𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗚𝗲𝗼𝗔𝗜 𝗛𝗮𝗰𝗸𝗮𝘁𝗵𝗼𝗻
🥇 1st Position – Sparsh Jain, Ujjwal Kakar, Akansh Gupta, Ridhwan Deshwal
Developed a neuro-symbolic pipeline for high-precision DTMs and optimized rural drainage layouts using hydrological modeling and genetic optimization.

🥉 3rd Position – Ishan Chugh , Rudransh Singh Rathore, Vanshika D., Vivaan Jain
Used PILNNs and DRL to identify waterlogging hotspots and generate gravity-compliant drainage designs.


𝗙𝗜𝗡𝗔𝗚𝗘𝗡𝗧 – 𝗝𝗶𝗼 𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗮𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝗦𝗲𝗿𝘃𝗶𝗰𝗲𝘀
🥉 3rd Position – Raj Aryan, Tarun Jain, Shreshth Rai, Sarthak Verma
Built SentinAL, a secure browser-based financial action agent with human-in-the-loop approval for safe execution of real banking tasks.


𝗣𝗮𝗰𝗸𝗲𝗱𝗴𝗲
🥈 2nd Position – Ishansh Sharma, Shivaay Dhondiyal, sarthak pandey, Prathamesh Bhatta, Vanshika D.
Redesigned Motion Chair packaging using an engineered L-shaped carton, cutting 45% internal void, doubling logistics density, and reducing cost by ₹107/unit while maintaining zero-tool assembly.

A big thanks to Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay for hosting a challenging and enriching fest.
